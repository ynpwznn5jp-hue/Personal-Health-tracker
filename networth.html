Edit assumptions

Saving creates a new assumption set that starts today. Earlier days keep the numbers they were recorded with. Stock fields show today's estimated value; if you change one, its new value becomes the baseline at the latest S&P 500 close. Home values you change are pinned to the latest Zillow index month.

Automatic updates

Runs four times a day (Central standard time): 7:15 am starts today's row from the last close, 3:35 pm records today's close, and 5:45 pm and 9:15 pm double-check it. During daylight time each run is an hour later. Weekends and holidays carry forward the last market close and show its date.

How the estimate works

(Trust home × Zillow change + trust cash + trust stocks × S&P change) × your trust share, plus your home × Zillow change minus the mortgage, plus personal stocks × S&P change, plus gold ounces × purity × gold price.

Stocks follow the S&P 500 price index from the Nov 3, 2026 close of 7,568.92. That leaves out dividends, fees and your actual holdings. Gold uses COMEX front-month futures closes, which track spot closely but are not the same price. Homes move with Zillow's monthly ZIP index (95060 and 78610), not with a property-specific estimate, so home values change once a month. Cash and mortgage stay fixed until you edit them. The days before Nov 4, 2026 were reconstructed from these real market and index values using today's assumptions, so they show how the same holdings would have moved, not records kept at the time. This is a rough estimate, not a legal valuation of the trust interest.
